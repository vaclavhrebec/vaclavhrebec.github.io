About

Václav Hřebec is a Czech music theorist and organist based in Basel, Switzerland. He studied classical guitar at the conservatories in Pilsen and Prague. His interest in early music brought him to Basel in 2023, where he is currently pursuing a Master’s degree in historical music theory at the Schola Cantorum Basiliensis, focusing on contrapunto alla mente and improvisation, alongside his studies in organ and lute. He works as a church organist and gives concerts, lectures, and workshops on early music.

Projects

The Ultimate Counterpoint Gym

Three rooms in the browser: Cantare super librum, with plainchant and mensural melodies to sing and improvise against; Improvising canons, where your own voice answers you as the comes; and the Guidonian hand, which plays the notes you show on your hand to the camera.

Enter the gym

The Ultimate Ear Trainer

Six rooms for the ear: intervals, intonation down to half a cent, key signatures in five clefs, graded rhythm reading and dictation, chords from triads to the Prometheus chord, and scales from the church modes to Arabic maqamat with quarter tones. In English, German, French and Czech.

Start training

Renaissance Music Course Rheinfelden

22–28 February 2027 · Reformed Church, Rheinfelden. A full week dedicated to the music of the 15th and 16th centuries: vocal and instrumental polyphony, cantare super librum, organ and lute improvisation. Course fee CHF 270 (reduced CHF 180).

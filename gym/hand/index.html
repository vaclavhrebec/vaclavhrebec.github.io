How to show a note

Hold your left hand up with the palm towards the camera. Touch a joint of a finger with the tip of your thumb: the base of each finger, its two middle joints, its tip.

The three notes on the thumb itself (Γ ut, A re, B mi) you show the other way round: touch the thumb with the tip of your curled index finger.

ee la sits on the back of the middle finger: curl it and touch its nail with the thumb.

Two hands

Turn the phone sideways, choose Two, and show both palms. Each hand is a voice of its own. The right hand carries the same gamut as a mirror image: the left-hand spiral turns anticlockwise, the right-hand spiral clockwise.

If the app mixes up two neighbouring places, press Teach my hand and show each position once. It then knows your hand instead of an average one, and it remembers it on this device.

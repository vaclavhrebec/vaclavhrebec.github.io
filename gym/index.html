The Ultimate Counterpoint Gym

Counterpoint is not dead.

I

Cantare super librum

Plainchant and mensural melodies in their original notation, with playback, transformations, talea, and choir-book layout, to sing and improvise against.

Open
II

Improvising canons

Sing the dux and hear yourself answered: the comes follows at the unison, fourth, fifth or octave, or in steps of the scale (seconds, thirds, sixths, sevenths).

Open
III

Guidonian hand

Show a joint of the hand to the camera, and the gym recognises it and sings the note back to you. One hand, or both at once in two voices.

Open
Also new: Ear Training, with intervals, intonation, key signatures, rhythm, chords and scales. →

Salamander Grand Piano V3 (Yamaha C5), recorded by Alexander Holm.

Source: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander (mp3 versions, one note every minor third).
The files are named by MIDI note number. map.json gives the measured deviation of each recording from
equal temperament in cents (c), so every note can be retuned exactly.
